I am a Ph.D. researcher in Information Systems (AI/ML Track) at the [University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC)](https://www.umbc.edu/), where I work in the [Mobile, Pervasive and Sensor Computing (MPSC) Lab](https://mpsc.umbc.edu/) with Prof. Nirmalya Roy.

My research builds **reliable, deployable, and evidence-grounded multimodal AI** for contactless physiological sensing, trustworthy medical reasoning, and autonomous health monitoring. I combine computer vision, video and time-series modeling, vision-language models, signal processing, uncertainty and reliability estimation, and on-device edge inference. My work spans algorithm development, peer-reviewed publications, field demonstrations, and testbed validation — including collaborative experiments with the DEVCOM Army Research Laboratory (ARL) and NSF-supported research infrastructure at UMBC.

Before UMBC, I was a graduate research assistant in the QUEST Lab at UNC Charlotte, a research assistant at the University of Hyogo in Japan, and a Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at Leading University, Bangladesh.

**Research interests**

- **Contactless physiological sensing** — respiratory and vital-sign monitoring from RGB / NIR / IR video, robust to motion, viewpoint, lighting, and occlusion
- **Trustworthy, evidence-grounded reasoning** — vision-language models that calibrate, verify, and audit their evidence before concluding
- **Multimodal foundation models and agentic health AI** — video–time-series alignment, low-data adaptation, and agentic perception-to-reporting workflows
- **Efficient edge and robotic sensing** — on-device inference on NVIDIA Jetson and mobile robots
