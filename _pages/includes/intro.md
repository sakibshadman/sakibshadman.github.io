I am a Ph.D. student in Information Systems at the [University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC)](https://umbc.edu/), specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I am a Graduate Research Assistant in the [Mobile Pervasive and Sensor Computing (MPSC) Lab](https://mpsc.umbc.edu/home), advised by [Prof. Nirmalya Roy](https://artiamas.umd.edu/nirmalya-roy/), and a Graduate Teaching Assistant with [Prof. Zhiyuan Chen](https://userpages.umbc.edu/~zhchen/).

My research focuses on **trustworthy multimodal AI for healthcare**. I develop methods for **contactless physiological monitoring**, estimating breathing and other vital signs from ordinary video and multimodal sensor data, using computer vision, time-series modeling, and vision-language models. A central question in my work is how AI systems use physiological evidence: I study where vision-language models and LLM-based agents fail, and make their outputs more reliable through calibration, evidence-grounded reasoning, uncertainty-aware inference, and counterfactual auditing, with particular attention to separating failures of perception, insufficient evidence, and reasoning.

I also care about getting these systems out of the lab. My work extends to efficient on-device inference on edge hardware and mobile robots, including field experiments with the DEVCOM Army Research Laboratory and demonstrations on NSF-supported testbeds at UMBC.

Previously, I was a research assistant at the [Advanced Medical Engineering Research Institute (AMERI)](https://ame-hyogo.org/english/), [University of Hyogo](https://www.u-hyogo.ac.jp/english/), Japan, as a [HUMAP Fellow](https://hyogo-humap.jp/en/search/203), working with [Dr. Syoji Kobashi](https://sites.google.com/site/syojikobashi/people/syojikobashi) and [Dr. Md Belayat Hossain](https://academics.siu.edu/computing-and-technology/computer-science/faculty/hossain-mdbelayat.php) on medical image processing and surgical video understanding. I have also worked as a Graduate Research Assistant in the [QUEST Lab](https://coefs.charlotte.edu/fdeeba/) at the [University of North Carolina at Charlotte](https://www.charlotte.edu/), and spent two years as a Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at [Leading University](https://www.lus.ac.bd/), Bangladesh.
