I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Information Systems at the [University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC)](https://umbc.edu/), specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I am a Graduate Research Assistant in the [Mobile Pervasive and Sensor Computing (MPSC) Lab](https://mpsc.umbc.edu/home), where I work with [Dr. Nirmalya Roy](https://artiamas.umd.edu/nirmalya-roy/). I also serve as a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Department of Information Systems with [Prof. Zhiyuan Chen](https://userpages.umbc.edu/~zhchen/).

My research focuses on **AI for health, multimodal physiological sensing, and trustworthy multimodal intelligence**. I develop methods for **contactless physiological monitoring** from video and multimodal sensor data, combining **computer vision, video and time-series modeling, multimodal deep learning, vision-language models, biomedical signal processing, uncertainty estimation, and edge AI**.

A major direction of my current work is **reasoning and safety in multimodal and language-model-based health systems**. I study how vision-language models and LLM-based agents use evidence, where they fail, and how their outputs can be made more reliable through **evidence-grounded reasoning, calibration, uncertainty-aware inference, selective and counterfactual auditing, active evidence acquisition, temporal reasoning, hallucination analysis, and LLM safety**. I am particularly interested in distinguishing failures caused by perception, insufficient evidence, and downstream reasoning.

I also explore **agentic and foundation-model-based physiological AI**, where multimodal models coordinate perception, evidence assessment, reasoning, verification, and reporting across visual, physiological, and contextual information. Alongside this, I work on robust contactless respiratory and vital-sign monitoring, quality-aware sensing, multimodal representation learning, and efficient deployment on edge and robotic platforms.

Previously, I conducted research at the [Advanced Medical Engineering Research Institute (AMERI)](https://ame-hyogo.org/english/) at the [University of Hyogo](https://www.u-hyogo.ac.jp/english/), Japan, as a recipient of the [HUMAP Fellowship](https://hyogo-humap.jp/en/search/203). There, I worked with [Dr. Syoji Kobashi](https://sites.google.com/site/syojikobashi/people/syojikobashi) and [Dr. Md Belayat Hossain](https://academics.siu.edu/computing-and-technology/computer-science/faculty/hossain-mdbelayat.php) on deep learning for medical image processing and surgical video understanding. I also worked as a Graduate Research Assistant in the [QUEST Lab](https://coefs.charlotte.edu/fdeeba/) at the [University of North Carolina at Charlotte](https://www.charlotte.edu/).

Before beginning my Ph.D., I spent two years as a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at [Leading University](https://www.lus.ac.bd/), Bangladesh, where I also served as a mentor for the IEEE Leading University Student Branch.
