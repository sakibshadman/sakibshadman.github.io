I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Information Systems at the [University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC)](https://umbc.edu/), specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I am a Graduate Research Assistant in the [Mobile Pervasive and Sensor Computing (MPSC) Lab](https://mpsc.umbc.edu/home), where I work with [Prof. Nirmalya Roy](https://artiamas.umd.edu/nirmalya-roy/).

My research lies at the intersection of **AI for health, multimodal physiological sensing, and trustworthy intelligent systems**. I develop learning-based methods for **contactless physiological monitoring**, with a particular focus on extracting and reasoning over health-related signals from video and multimodal sensor data. My work brings together **computer vision, multimodal deep learning, vision-language models, biomedical signal processing, and sensor-based computing** to build health AI systems that are accurate, reliable, and deployable in real-world environments.

My recent research focuses on contactless respiratory and vital-sign monitoring, including multimodal representation learning, temporal and frequency-domain modeling, reliability-aware learning, and vision-language reasoning for physiological signals. More broadly, I am interested in **medical image analysis, computer-aided diagnosis, disease detection and prediction, and efficient and trustworthy AI for healthcare**.

Previously, I conducted research at the [Advanced Medical Engineering Research Institute (AMERI)](https://ame-hyogo.org/english/) at the [University of Hyogo](https://www.u-hyogo.ac.jp/english/), Japan, as a recipient of the [HUMAP Fellowship](https://hyogo-humap.jp/en/search/203). There, I worked with [Prof. Syoji Kobashi](https://sites.google.com/site/syojikobashi/people/syojikobashi) and [Prof. Md Belayat Hossain](https://academics.siu.edu/computing-and-technology/computer-science/faculty/hossain-mdbelayat.php) on deep learning for medical image processing and surgical video analysis. I also worked as a Graduate Research Assistant in the [QUEST Lab](https://coefs.charlotte.edu/fdeeba/) at the [University of North Carolina at Charlotte](https://www.charlotte.edu/).

Before beginning my Ph.D., I spent two years as a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at [Leading University](https://www.lus.ac.bd/), Bangladesh, where I also served as a mentor for the IEEE Leading University Student Branch. I received my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering with distinction from the [International University of Business Agriculture and Technology (IUBAT)](https://iubat.edu/), Bangladesh.
