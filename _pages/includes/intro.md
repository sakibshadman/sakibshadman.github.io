I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Information Systems at the [University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC)](https://umbc.edu/), working on **trustworthy multimodal AI for healthcare**. I am advised by [Prof. Nirmalya Roy](https://artiamas.umd.edu/nirmalya-roy/) in the [Mobile Pervasive and Sensor Computing (MPSC) Lab](https://mpsc.umbc.edu/home), am affiliated with the [Center for Research in Use-Inspired Cyber-Physical Systems (CYPRESS)](https://cypress.umbc.edu/), and serve as a Graduate Teaching Assistant with [Prof. Zhiyuan Chen](https://userpages.umbc.edu/~zhchen/).

My research develops methods for **contactless physiological monitoring**, estimating breathing and other vital signs from ordinary video and multimodal sensor data, and studies where vision-language models and LLM-based agents fail, making their outputs more reliable through calibration, evidence-grounded reasoning, and counterfactual auditing. My work has appeared at leading computer-vision, machine-learning, and health-informatics venues and their workshops, including **CVPR, WACV, AAAI, ICML, NeurIPS, MICCAI, BMVC, ACM CIKM, ACM CHASE, and ICMLA**. I also work to move these systems out of the lab, through efficient on-device inference on edge hardware and mobile robots, field experiments with the DEVCOM Army Research Laboratory, and demonstrations on the NSF-supported CYPRESS testbeds.

Previously, I was a [HUMAP Fellow](https://hyogo-humap.jp/en/search/203) at the [Advanced Medical Engineering Research Institute (AMERI)](https://ame-hyogo.org/english/), [University of Hyogo](https://www.u-hyogo.ac.jp/english/), Japan, working with [Prof. Syoji Kobashi](https://sites.google.com/site/syojikobashi/people/syojikobashi) and [Prof. Md Belayat Hossain](https://academics.siu.edu/computing-and-technology/computer-science/faculty/hossain-mdbelayat.php) on medical image and surgical video analysis; a Graduate Research Assistant in the [QUEST Lab](https://coefs.charlotte.edu/fdeeba/) at the [University of North Carolina at Charlotte](https://www.charlotte.edu/); and a Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at [Leading University](https://www.lus.ac.bd/), Bangladesh.
