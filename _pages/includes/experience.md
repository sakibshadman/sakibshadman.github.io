# 🔬 Research Experience

### Graduate Research Assistant
**[Mobile, Pervasive and Sensor Computing (MPSC) Lab](https://mpsc.umbc.edu/), University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC)**  
*Jan. 2024 – Present · Baltimore, Maryland*

Working with **Dr. Nirmalya Roy** on reliable, multimodal, and trustworthy AI for physiological sensing and health monitoring.

- **Contactless physiological sensing:** developing robust respiratory and vital-sign monitoring methods from RGB, NIR, and IR video, including multimodal temporal-frequency modeling, waveform reconstruction, quality-aware sensing, and reliability-guided representation learning.
- **Trustworthy multimodal reasoning:** investigating vision-language models and health agents that reason over physiological evidence using calibration, uncertainty estimation, evidence grounding, active evidence acquisition, selective auditing, and counterfactual evaluation.
- **Foundation and agentic models for health:** exploring multimodal foundation models that coordinate perception, evidence assessment, reasoning, verification, and reporting across visual and physiological signals.
- **Edge and real-world deployment:** translating sensing and reasoning pipelines to resource-constrained and robotic platforms for practical health monitoring.

Selected research systems include **RespFormer, E2RespUNet, RRPIPS, VLS-RR, CALIBRA, EviCal-RR, and ViTaL-Diff**.
